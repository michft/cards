Top Solitaire Games by Popularity

    Klondike: The standard, most popular version; involves building foundations by suit from Ace to King.
    Spider: A challenging two-deck game, often considered more skill-intensive, popular for its ability to overcome luck.
    FreeCell: Known for being highly solvable (most deals can be won) because most cards are revealed early.
    Pyramid: A game involving pairing cards to remove them from a pyramid structure, popular in modern collections.
    Tri-Peaks: Involves clearing peaks by selecting cards that are one higher or lower than the top card on the waste pile.
    Yukon: Similar to Klondike but allows moving entire stacks, even if they aren't in sequence, allowing for more strategic play.
    Scorpion: A builder game similar to Spider that allows for moving stacks within the tableau.
